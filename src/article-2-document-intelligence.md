## Why Document Processing Is Still Stuck in the 1990s

Every operation runs on documents. Invoices, contracts, purchase orders, IDs, applications, correspondence — the paper (and PDF) trail is the connective tissue of business. Yet for most teams, handling that flow means one thing: people. Staff manually opening files, reading them, extracting key data points, and entering them into systems of record — a process that is slow, expensive, and error-prone at scale.

The result is a hidden tax on every department: finance re-keying invoices, operations typing order details, compliance reviewing documents by hand, and support teams copying data between systems.

**Document Intelligence (IDP)** changes the economics of that work entirely.

## What Is Intelligent Document Processing (IDP)?

Intelligent Document Processing combines several AI technologies to read, understand, and extract structured data from unstructured documents — automatically, at scale, and with accuracy that rivals (and often exceeds) manual review.

A complete IDP stack includes:

- **OCR (Optical Character Recognition):** Converts scanned images and PDFs into machine-readable text — even handwriting and low-quality scans
- **Document Classification Models:** Trained on document corpora to recognize and route documents by type with high confidence
- **NLP and Data Extraction Models:** Pull structured fields (names, amounts, dates, reference numbers) from unstructured text
- **Validation and Enrichment Logic:** Cross-checks extracted data against your existing systems and flags anomalies
- **Human-in-the-Loop Review:** Routes low-confidence documents to a human for quick correction while high-confidence documents flow through untouched

## What Document Intelligence Handles Across the Business

A well-deployed IDP system handles the full spectrum of business documents without requiring manual routing:

### Finance & Accounts Payable

- Invoices are captured from email, scanned, and matched to purchase orders
- Line items, totals, taxes, and vendor details are extracted and posted automatically
- Three-way matching exceptions are flagged for review

### Sales & Customer Operations

- Orders and contracts are parsed for terms, pricing, and renewal dates
- Customer IDs and KYC documents are verified instantly
- Onboarding paperwork moves through approval without re-keying

### HR & Compliance

- Resumes and applications are parsed and matched against job requirements
- Certificates and compliance documents are validated and filed
- Audit trails are generated automatically from ingestion to storage

### Procurement & Logistics

- Bills of lading, packing slips, and delivery notes are captured at intake
- Quantities and references are reconciled automatically against systems
- Exceptions route to the right person with full context

## How Document Intelligence Works in a Live Environment

A document arrives — by email, scan, fax, or upload. From that moment, no human touches it:

1. **Capture:** The document is ingested from any channel and converted to a searchable format
2. **Classify:** AI identifies the document type — invoice, contract, ID, form — in milliseconds
3. **Extract:** Field-level data is pulled with confidence scores per field
4. **Validate:** Extracted data is checked against existing records; mismatches are flagged
5. **Route:** The document and its data flow into your ERP, CRM, or workflow with zero re-keying
6. **Learn:** Corrections made in review feed back to improve accuracy over time

## Common Integration Points for Document AI

The value of IDP multiplies when it's connected to the systems your team already uses:

- **CRM and help desks** — attachments become structured records and tickets
- **ERP and accounting systems** — invoices flow straight to posting and approval
- **Content and file systems** — documents are classified, named, and filed automatically
- **Workflow platforms** — extracted data triggers the next step in the process automatically

Integration is typically done through clean REST APIs and webhooks, so your existing stack stays intact.
